QUESTIONS?

Does my photo get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything — reading and stripping — happens in your browser with plain JavaScript. There is no server, no analytics on your files, no account. Turn off your Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.

Does stripping reduce image quality?

No. For JPEG and PNG, MetaStrip removes metadata segments byte-by-byte without touching the compressed pixel data — the image itself is bit-for-bit identical. Other formats fall back to a high-quality re-encode.

Don't Instagram / WhatsApp already strip metadata?

Big social platforms mostly do — but email, cloud drives, marketplaces, forums, blogs, and many messaging apps don't. If you're selling something on a classifieds site with photos taken at home, your address may be attached.

What formats are supported?

JPEG and PNG get full metadata inspection and lossless stripping. Other browser-readable images (WebP, AVIF, etc.) are cleaned via canvas re-encode.

Is it open source?

Yes — the whole thing is a few hundred lines of dependency-free JavaScript. Read every byte on GitHub.
